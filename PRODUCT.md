# Fabric bar

## Register
product

## Purpose
Top status bar for an i3/X11 desktop on two 1920x1080 monitors. Glanced at dozens of times an hour; must answer "which workspace, what time, is the machine OK, is audio going where I think" in under a second, then get out of the way.

## Users
One developer, dark room and daylight, Everforest-themed desktop with a photographic wallpaper behind the bar.

## Personality
Quiet, precise, calm. Modern minimalism: the wallpaper stays visible, the bar reads as three small surfaces, not a strip of widgets.

## Principles
1. Color means state. Icons and labels are neutral; green = focused workspace, red = overheat or muted, cyan = caps lock. Nothing is colored for decoration.
2. Values first, details on hover. Always visible: temp, cpu, ram used, date/time, workspaces, layout, net speeds, volume. On hover: RAM total, IP and interface, volume slider.
3. Three islands (left, center, right), modules separated by space, not dividers.
4. Numbers never jitter: tabular/fixed-width values.
5. Keep the polybar module order and the Everforest palette (colors.ini).

## Anti-references
Rainbow per-module icon colors, powerline caps, divider pipes between every item, glass blur, heavy borders + shadows.
